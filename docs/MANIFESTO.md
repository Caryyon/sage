# The Future of AI is Distributed

## The Problem

AI today is a feudal system.

A handful of corporations control the largest models. They decide what you can ask, what answers you get, and how much you pay. Your conversations are logged, analyzed, and fed back into training data you never consented to. The "free tier" is a data extraction scheme with better marketing.

This is not a bug. It's the business model.

## The Alternative

What if AI worked like the internet itself — decentralized, resilient, no single point of control?

What if your AI learned from you, privately, on your own machine?
What if it could share knowledge with others without sharing your data?
What if it got smarter not by draining your wallet, but by connecting to a mesh of peers?

## This is SAGE.

SAGE is **BitTorrent for intelligence**.

Knowledge is encoded into Neural Cellular Automata patterns — compressed neural representations that can't be reversed into original text. These patterns sync peer-to-peer across a mesh network. The more nodes join, the smarter the collective intelligence becomes.

Your raw conversations never leave your machine. Only encoded, anonymous patterns are shared.

## The Technology

SAGE stores knowledge in a **256×256 Neural Cellular Automata grid** — not a database, not a vector store, but a living neural structure.

- **Encoding**: Text → semantic embeddings → NCA grid patterns (kilobytes, not gigabytes)
- **Retrieval**: Cross-attention decoder reads grid cells based on query relevance
- **Sync**: Gossip protocol exchanges Merkle-verified diffs between peers
- **Security**: Ed25519 cryptographic signatures on all knowledge transfers
- **Privacy**: Only encoded neural patterns sync — original text stays local
